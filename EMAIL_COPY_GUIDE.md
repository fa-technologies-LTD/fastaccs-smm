# Fast Accounts email voice

## Audience

Write for mobile-first customers in Nigeria who buy social accounts, verification numbers, or boosting services for personal use, work, or resale.

They usually want to know four things quickly:

1. What happened?
2. Is my money or order safe?
3. What do I need to do?
4. Where can I get help?

## Voice

- Plain, confident, and respectful
- Friendly without sounding childish
- Professional without corporate language
- Short sentences and familiar words
- Naira amounts formatted clearly
- Honest about timing, stock, and order status

## Structure

- Subject: outcome or required action, ideally under 55 characters
- Heading: same promise as the subject; do not invent a second message
- First line: the current status
- Middle: only the important amount, reference, timing, or instructions
- Button: one clear action, two to five words
- Support: handled by the shared footer, not repeated in the body

## Avoid

- Long introductions or repeated thank-yous
- Technical terms such as webhook, provider payload, reconciliation, or fulfilment lease
- Unverified urgency or scarcity
- Hard-coded prices in automated campaigns
- More than one emoji in a subject
- Multiple competing buttons
- Slang that may reduce trust around payments or account security
- Promises such as “instant” when a service can legitimately take longer

## Preferred wording

- `Payment received` instead of `Your transaction has been successfully processed`
- `Your order is ready` instead of `We are delighted to inform you`
- `Update your link` instead of `The supplied URL failed validation`
- `Refunded to store credit` instead of `A reversal has been initiated`
- `View order` instead of `Click here to proceed`
- `Need help?` instead of a long support paragraph

## Final review

Before shipping an email, check that:

- The subject is truthful and useful on a phone lock screen.
- The most important status appears in the first two lines.
- Any price, date, quantity, or payout rule comes from live data/configuration.
- The body has one main action.
- The CTA destination matches the wording.
- Transactional and marketing consent rules remain correct.
- Customer-controlled values are escaped by the shared renderer.
